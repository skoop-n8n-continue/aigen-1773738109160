Tagging Workflow Guide

A comprehensive tutorial for managing tags on Skoopsignage

Understanding Tags in Digital Signage

In a complex digital signage network, organization is key. Tags provide a flexible, dynamic way to manage where your content plays.

The Concept: Instead of manually assigning every piece of content to individual screens, you can assign Tags to both Screens and Playlist Items. When a screen checks for content, it can automatically play playlist items that share its assigned tags. For example, a screen tagged with "Ground Floor" will automatically pick up and play any promotional content also tagged with "Ground Floor". This makes scaling and targeting content incredibly efficient.

1

Login to the Dashboard

Start by navigating to cloud.skoopsignage.com and logging in with your credentials.

2

Create New Tags

Before you can assign tags, you need to create them. Navigate to the Settings menu on the left panel, and click on the Tags tab. Here, you can type your tag names (e.g., "Ground Floor", "Promo", "Summer Sale") and click Create to add them to your global tag list.

3

Assign Tags to a Screen

Now, let's tag a physical display. Go to the Screens section from the left panel. Locate your target screen (e.g., "Pro skoop stick home ground floor").

Click the three-dot menu icon on the right side of the screen's row, select Details. The Screen Details panel will appear on the right side. Scroll down to expand the Screen Tags accordion section. You can now search for and add the tags you created earlier.

4

Assign Tags to Playlist Items

Finally, we tag the content. Navigate to the Playlists section and open your target playlist (e.g., "Smart Tags").

Find an item in your playlist, click the small Tag icon (it looks like a price tag) associated with it. This will open the Tags popup modal where you can search and assign relevant tags.

Once saved, you can simply hover over the tag icon on the item to quickly see a tooltip displaying which tags are attached to it.
